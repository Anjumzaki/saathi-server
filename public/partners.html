Partners

Saathi works better when it's already in the room.

Clinics that hand out plans, employers that pay for wellbeing, and the bodies that certify dietitians. Three ways to work with us, none of them a white-label.

Clinics & hospitals

Your dietitians get Saathi Pro seats and a clinic referral code. Patients who install with it skip the marketplace and land on your team. You see adherence per patient, never their private data.

  • Pro seats billed to the clinic
  • Referral code at discharge
  • Adherence dashboard (effort, swaps, missed)

Employers & insurers

Premium for a team, paid centrally. Anonymised, aggregated reporting only: how many people are on a plan, average effort, nothing per person. Focus hours are pre-filled from your working hours.

  • Per-seat Premium, monthly
  • Aggregate reporting, minimum group of 20
  • Dietitian credits as a benefit

Dietitian bodies & universities

Verify your members once, and their Saathi Pro badge shows your body's name. Students get a supervised tier to build plans under a registered mentor.

  • Registry integration for verification
  • Co-branded verified badge
  • Student tier with mentor sign-off
What we don't do

No white-label, no data resale

Saathi stays Saathi in the user's hands, because the mascot and the reminder behaviour are the product. Partners never see cycle, Together or chat content, and we never sell or share user data with partners beyond the aggregates described above.
