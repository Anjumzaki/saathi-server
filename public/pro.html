How it works

From request to recap

1

A request arrives

With her intake already filled: goal, kitchen, cooking time, work hours, sleep. Never her cycle. Reply within 24 h to keep your Top match badge.

2

Build from a template

Your own templates or the Saathi starter. Live structural check as you type: gaps, protein at each main, tea away from iron, dinner cutoff. Your numbers, if any, stay yours.

3

Publish with a diff

She sees exactly what changed and keeps her reminder times where they match. Updates later work the same way, from chat.

4

Watch it land

Her logs, swaps and photos in chat. A 15-minute call on your own Meet or WhatsApp link, booked around her quiet and focus hours.

Stay in the loop

Updates that reach her reminders, not her inbox

Every change you send becomes a diff she reviews once. Photos she logs appear in your chat with the meal they replaced. You see effort, swaps and streaks; you never see cycle or Together data.
