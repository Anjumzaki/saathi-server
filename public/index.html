Swap, don't cheat

Life happens. The plan bends.

Swaps come from your own usual foods, in the same category as the meal you missed. Saathi logs it as "something else" so your dietitian sees it too. There is no red anywhere in this app.

Cycle-aware

It knows which week it is. Nobody else does.

Seed cycling cards change with your phase. A monthly check-in asks one question. Together, the module for the two of you, shows on the lock screen as a heart and nothing more. Cycle data is never shown to a dietitian or a partner.

Progress that encourages

An effort score, not a verdict

The ring fills with what you did, not what you weighed. Sunday brings a one-minute recap and one small thing for next week. Missed days say "fresh start tomorrow", and mean it.

Movement, without becoming a gym app

A walk after the meal. Strength and yoga, slotted in for you.

Saathi already knows your meals, your focus hours, your prayer times and your bedtime. So it plans the movement around them, instead of asking you to.

A walk after each meal

Ten minutes after you mark breakfast, lunch or dinner done. Fifteen to twenty-five minutes, depending on how far you are from 8,000 or 10,000 steps. Never in your focus hours, never in the hour before bed.

Three strength, two yoga

A week at a time, on non-consecutive days. Saathi finds a free slot and proposes it; you move it if it doesn't suit. Skip three in a row and it offers a different time instead of repeating itself.

Yoga for the stressful days

Log a stressful day and a short calming session appears, with a breathing tip for everyone. Premium turns a session into illustrated step-by-step cards with a timer. Lighter sessions during your period.

BMI free, one target if you need it

BMI sits live on the weight screen. If weight loss is part of your goal, Premium adds a single daily calorie target at a steady half a kilo a week — never below 1,200, switched off entirely for a low BMI or a recovery goal. Still no calorie logging.

Reliability, honestly

What rings, on which phone

On Android, meals and wake use full-screen exact alarms, and a setup wizard walks you through the battery settings on Xiaomi, Samsung, Oppo and Vivo. On iOS, reminders arrive as time-sensitive notifications; iOS doesn't let apps show full-screen alarms, so we don't claim it.
