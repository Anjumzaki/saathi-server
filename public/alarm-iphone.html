Alarm reliability · iOS 16 and later

Saathi alarms not ringing on iPhone? Fix it in two minutes.

iOS does not let any third-party app show a full-screen alarm over the lock screen. Only Apple's own Clock app can. So Saathi uses time-sensitive notifications with a repeating sound, and says so rather than pretending otherwise.

Do these, in order

  1. Settings › Saathi › Notifications → Allow, with Time Sensitive on and Lock Screen on
  2. Settings › Focus › each Focus you use › Apps → add Saathi so reminders break through
  3. Settings › Screen Time › Downtime → add Saathi to Always Allowed
  4. Keep Low Power Mode off overnight if you rely on the wake reminder

Steps come from your watch or iPhone only once Health integration ships; until then the step count rises while the app is open. A walk can always be marked done by hand.

Then test it, don't hope

Open Saathi › Settings › Make reminders reliable › Try a ring now. Lock the phone first: an alarm that rings with the screen on proves nothing. If it rings over the lock screen, you're done.

If it still misses

  1. Check the phone's own battery saver is not on a schedule overnight.
  2. Check Do Not Disturb: meal alarms bypass it, soft nudges don't, by design.
  3. Reboot, then confirm the first alarm after the reboot rings — that's the one autostart affects.
  4. Still wrong? Write to support@saathi-app.com with your model and Android version and we'll walk through it.

Why Saathi cares so much

A diet plan dies the day a meal reminder gets swiped away. That's why meals use the alarm channel instead of a normal notification — and why the app checks these settings on the first run instead of leaving you to find this page.
