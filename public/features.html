It rings like an alarm

  • Snooze ladder: 10 min × 3, then Skip
  • Works offline; power-cut mode where outages are common

Quiet times & fasting

Set your own quiet windows for prayer, a nap, a night feed or a class, and choose for each one whether reminders wait, go silent or still ring. Tell Saathi the days you fast and it follows dawn and sunset instead of offering a meal you are not eating. An alarm you set yourself always rings.

A walk after each meal

Ten minutes after you mark breakfast, lunch or dinner done, Saathi suggests a walk. Not a goal to hit someday — a walk now, while you're already up.

  • 15, 20 or 25 minutes, from how far you are off your step goal
  • Delayed by focus hours and prayer times; none in the hour before bed
  • Hot, raining, bad air or after dark → your indoor option instead, and it says why
  • Completes itself from the step count, or one tap by hand
  • Works with any plan's meal names, Iftar and Sehri included

Strength and yoga, slotted into your week

Three short strength sessions for weight, two yoga sessions for stress. You don't pick the times; Saathi finds gaps that don't collide with the rest of your day and proposes them.

  • Strength on non-consecutive days, same time each day where possible
  • Never in focus hours, never within an hour of a meal, no strength before bed
  • Lighter and capped at 15 minutes during your period
  • Skip three in a row and it proposes a different time, at least an hour away
  • Sessions start at 10–15 minutes and grow by five after every four done
  • Premium: the routine as illustrated step-by-step cards with a timer
  • Log a stressful day and a calming session appears; a breathing tip for everyone

Save my week

BMI free. One number, only if you need it.

No calorie logging anywhere in Saathi — no photographing plates, no looking up a biryani. The single calorie figure in the app is a daily target, and most people never see it.

  • BMI live on the weight screen, with the standard ranges, free
  • A daily target only when weight loss is part of your goal, free
  • One pace: half a kilo a week, recalculated each time you log a weight
  • Never below 1,200 kcal; withheld for a low BMI, recovery or conceive goals
  • A dietitian's numbers always take priority over ours
