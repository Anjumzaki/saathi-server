Alarms not ringing

My alarm didn't ring this morning

Nine times out of ten the phone's battery saver stopped Saathi overnight. Open Settings › Make reminders reliable › Fix now, or follow the steps for your phone: Xiaomi, Samsung, Oppo, Realme, Vivo, Infinix, Pixel, iPhone. Then tap Try a ring now.

The alarm rang but didn't show full screen

On Android 14+ full-screen notifications need a separate permission: Settings › Apps › Saathi › Notifications › Allow full-screen notifications. On iPhone, full-screen alarms aren't possible for any app; you'll get a time-sensitive notification with a repeating sound.

It rang during a meeting

Set focus hours in Settings › Focus hours, or pick "Day off" for today. Quiet hours (prayer times) also pause alarms if your region pack has them.

How many times can I snooze?

Three, ten minutes each. On the third the main button becomes Skip today and the alarm stops repeating.

Plans & dietitians

Can I use the plan my own doctor gave me?

Yes. Type it into "Write my own" and the structural check will tidy it, or enter the times by hand. Reading a PDF or photo with AI is a Premium feature that is coming; you'll confirm each line it reads. You can also check a plan on the website before you install anything.

How do I hire a dietitian?

The marketplace is coming, not live yet. When it opens: browse the directory in the app, open a profile, tap Request plan and pay through Google Play or the App Store. Payment is held until the plan is delivered, then released 24 hours later. Refund if nothing arrives within 72 hours.

What does my dietitian see?

Your goals, kitchen, days, plan, logs, swaps and chat. Never your cycle, Together days or allergies. Weight only if you share it.

Why is the follow-up discount only with the same dietitian?

Because the follow-up is cheaper to deliver: she already knows your plan. A new dietitian starts from scratch, so it's priced as a new plan.

Focus hours & quiet hours

What happens to reminders during focus hours?

Your choice of three: they become silent nudges, they wait and arrive as one catch-up card when focus ends, or meals still ring and the rest go quiet. Any single reminder can be marked "always rings".

Can I have more than one window?

Yes: a work window, an evening class, a shift. Add them in Settings › Focus hours.

Prayer times and focus overlap

The stricter rule wins. Nothing rings in either.

Walks, strength and yoga

Why am I asked to walk after a meal?

Ten minutes after you mark breakfast, lunch or dinner done, Saathi suggests 15 to 25 minutes, depending on how far you are from your step goal. If you never mark the meal done it asks once, 40 minutes after the meal time, and then leaves it.

It's 40 degrees outside. Can it stop telling me to walk?

It already does. Saathi checks your city's forecast and air quality and switches to your indoor option when it's too hot, too cold, raining, the air is bad, or it's after dark — and it tells you why. You can also turn the walk nudge off in Settings.

How do the strength and yoga sessions get scheduled?

Three strength and two yoga a week by default, which you can change. Saathi proposes slots that avoid your focus hours, prayer times, the hour after a meal and the hour before bed, and keeps them at the same time where it can. Miss three in a row and it proposes a different time.

Do I need Premium for them?

No. The slots, the reminders, the type and the duration are free. Premium turns a session into illustrated step-by-step cards with a timer.

I'm on my period

Sessions are lighter and capped at 15 minutes on cycle days 1 to 5, if you track your cycle.

Weight, BMI and calories

Does Saathi count calories?

There is no calorie logging anywhere: no photographing plates, no food database, no numbers on plan items or recipes. The only calorie figure is an optional daily target, and only if weight loss is part of your goal.

How is the daily target worked out?

From your height, weight, age and how active your days are, minus 500 kcal, rounded to the nearest 50, and recalculated each time you log a weight. It is never below 1,200 and is labelled as an estimate. A dietitian's number always takes priority.

Why can't I see a calorie target?

Because one of these applies: your goal isn't weight loss, your BMI is in the underweight range, your goal is PCOS recovery, post-pregnancy or trying to conceive, or your goal weight is at or above your current weight. In all of those a deficit isn't appropriate, so the app doesn't show one.

Where do I find my BMI?

Live on the height and weight screen while you set it, and on Progress under "Your body". It's free.

Premium & billing

How does the free month work?

Start Premium with no card. Three days before it ends you get one notification. If you do nothing you drop to Free and keep all your data.

Where do I cancel?

In your Google Play or App Store subscriptions. Access continues to the end of the paid period.

The price looks different from my friend's

Prices are set per country so Premium costs about the same share of a grocery bill everywhere. The app shows your country's price.

Account & data

How do I export or delete my data?

Settings › Export my data gives you a copy as a file. Settings › Delete my account removes the account and everything in it; Sign out & reset data clears your data but keeps the account. Backups age out within 90 days. Full detail, and how to request it without the app, is on Delete my account.

I got a new phone

Sign in and everything syncs back, including reminder times. Re-run Make reminders reliable on the new phone; every maker needs its own settings.

What shows on my lock screen?

"Reminder" by default. Together shows a heart, never words. Cycle check-ins never show content on the lock screen. Change it in Settings › Privacy & lock screen.

Is my cycle data shared with anyone?

No. Not with dietitians, and there is no partner or family view in Saathi for it to reach. Turn cycle tracking off and its history goes with it.
