Privacy policy

Plain words about what Saathi stores and who can see it.

Saathi is server-first with a copy on your phone. That lets a new phone, a dietitian update or a partner cheer just work. It also means we hold your data, so this page says exactly what, where and for how long.

What we collect

Account: name, email, phone (verified only when you book a call with a dietitian). Your world: goals, location (city level), language, units, kitchen, work pattern, sleep times. Plan: meals, supplements, reminder times, rules. Activity: what you logged, swapped or skipped, water, steps (counted on the phone, and, only if you switch it on, daily step totals read from Health Connect on Android or Apple Health on iPhone; Saathi reads them and never writes to either), walks and workout sessions you completed, weight if you enter it. Cycle: period dates and symptoms, only if you turn cycle tracking on.

We never compute calories from what you eat, and we never infer a diagnosis. Saathi is not a medical device.

Where it lives

On our servers (encrypted at rest and in transit) and in a full offline copy on your phone so alarms ring without signal. We run on a managed cloud platform; the hosting region will be named on this page before launch, and we will tell you in the app if it ever changes. Regional hosting for Pakistan, India and the GCC is on the roadmap.

Who can see what

  • You. Everything.
  • A dietitian you hire sees your goals, kitchen, days, plan, logs and swaps, plus anything you send in chat. Never cycle, Together, weight (unless you share it) or partner data.
  • Nobody else, by default. There is no partner or family view in Saathi, so there is nothing to switch on and nothing to leak. Allergies are never shared, even with a dietitian you hire.
  • Organisation partners (a clinic or employer that paid for your seat) see aggregates for groups of 20 or more. Never a person.
  • Saathi staff see data only to fix a problem you reported, with your ticket number, and it is logged.

Your lock screen

Notifications show "Reminder" by default. Together shows a heart. Cycle check-ins never show content on the lock screen regardless of your settings. You can choose to show more in Settings › Privacy & lock screen.

Read with AI

Read with AI is part of Premium. When you add a photo, a PDF or pasted text of a plan, it is sent through our server to an AI service run by Anthropic, which reads it and returns the meals, supplements and times. Saathi does not keep the document. Nothing is saved until you have checked the result and chosen to use it; what you keep is stored as your plan. A plan can include health information, so only add documents you are happy to have read this way. We do not train models on your photos or plans. AI can make mistakes, which is why you always review the result first.

How long we keep it

While your account exists. Sign out in Settings leaves your data in your account. Sign out & reset data erases your plan, logs and settings from the phone and the server but keeps the account. Delete my account removes the account and everything in it. In each case the copy on your phone goes immediately; backups age out within 90 days. Step-by-step instructions, and what we have to keep, are on Delete my account. Dietitians keep a record of plans they delivered to you (their professional obligation) with your name removed on request.

Your rights

Correct anything in Profile. Delete the account yourself, from the app or from this page. Export my data in Settings gives you a copy of what is on your phone as a file; ask us if you want the server copy as well. Email privacy@saathi-app.com for anything else; we answer within 30 days, usually much sooner.

Children

Saathi is for adults. We do not knowingly accept accounts from anyone under 18.

Changes

We will tell you in the app before a change that affects what we collect or share takes effect, and keep the previous version linked here.
